🛡️ CyberSuraksha AI

Defending Digital Citizens from Online Scams

🔗 Live Application:
👉 https://cybersuraksha-ai.streamlit.app/

CyberSuraksha AI is a web-based intelligent assistant designed to help digital citizens identify, understand, and protect themselves from online scams, phishing attempts, and malicious websites. The platform focuses on cyber awareness, explainability, and prevention, rather than blind automation.

This project was built as a Proof of Concept (PoC) for the iSafe Hackathon – Defend the Digital Citizen category.

🚨 Problem Statement

With increasing digital adoption, users—especially students and first-time internet users—are frequently exposed to:

Phishing messages and scam emails

Fake job offers and lottery scams

Malicious or deceptive websites

Social engineering attacks

Most existing cybersecurity tools are either too technical or inaccessible to non-expert users. There is a strong need for a simple, explainable, and user-friendly cyber safety assistant.

💡 Solution Overview

CyberSuraksha AI provides an intuitive interface where users can:

Analyze suspicious messages (SMS, emails, chat content)

Check potentially unsafe website URLs

Understand why something may be risky

Learn what actions to take to stay safe online

The system acts as a decision-support tool, empowering users with awareness rather than making absolute claims.

✨ Key Features
🔐 Authentication

Secure user authentication using Supabase

🔓 Demo Login option for judges and evaluators (no signup required)

📩 Scam & Phishing Message Analyzer

Paste any suspicious message or email

AI-powered risk classification: Low / Medium / High

Clear explanation of scam indicators

🌐 URL Scam Checker

Analyze suspicious website URLs

Detect common red flags and deceptive patterns

Safety guidance for users
